# Orders and changes

## Order statuses

An order goes through these statuses: placed (payment received), packed (picked in the warehouse, no changes possible anymore), shipped (handed to the carrier, tracking active), delivered, and, where relevant, returned or cancelled. The status is visible in your account and in the emails we send at each step.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can cancel or change an order yourself from your account as long as the status is still "placed". Because we dispatch quickly, that window is usually 30 to 60 minutes on working days. Once the status is "packed" or "shipped" the order can no longer be changed or cancelled; refuse the parcel at the door or return it after delivery using the normal 30 day return process. We cannot add items to an existing order; place a second order and, if both are still "placed", contact support and we combine the shipping fee.

## Changing the delivery address

The delivery address can be changed in your account while the status is "placed". After dispatch, PostNL and DHL let you redirect the parcel to a pickup point or another address in the same country through the tracking link. We cannot change the country of destination after an order is placed.

## Backorders and pre-orders

If an item shows "ships in 2 to 4 weeks" it is on backorder. You are charged at checkout and we email you when the item leaves the warehouse. Backordered items in a mixed order are shipped separately at no extra cost. You can cancel a backordered line at any time before it ships for a full refund of that line.

## Order confirmation and invoices

An order confirmation is sent within minutes of placing the order. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder and the email address on your account. The invoice is attached to the dispatch email and can be downloaded from the order page in your account.

## Wrong or missing items

If you received a wrong item or something is missing, contact support within 14 days of delivery with the order number and a photo of the packing slip. We send the missing or correct item right away and, for a wrong item, a prepaid label to send the incorrect one back.
