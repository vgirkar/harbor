# Warranty and repairs

## What is covered

Every product sold by Harbor Outfitters is covered against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty period depends on the category: backpacks and duffels carry a lifetime warranty, tents, sleeping bags and sleeping mats carry a five year warranty, apparel, footwear, headlamps and electronics carry a two year warranty, and accessories (bottles, cutlery, straps, stuff sacks) carry a one year warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date and stays with the product, not the owner, so second hand items are covered for the remaining period if the original order number is known.

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage (cuts, burns, falls), damage from incorrect care (see the product care guide), UV degradation after years of use, and modifications are not covered. Zips, buckles and elastic cords are wear parts and are covered for the first year only.

## How to make a warranty claim

Email support with your order number, two or three photos of the fault, and a short description of how and when it appeared. We respond within two working days. For most apparel and accessory faults we decide from the photos and ship a replacement without asking for the item back. For backpacks, tents and footwear we may ask you to send the item in; we send a prepaid label and the inspection takes up to ten working days from arrival.

Approved claims are resolved, in this order of preference: free repair, replacement with the same product, replacement with the nearest current model if the original is discontinued, or a refund of the purchase price if neither is possible. Repairs are done by our in-house workshop in Rotterdam or by the manufacturer.

## Repairs outside warranty

Our workshop also repairs items that are not covered, for example a torn pack after a fall or a jacket zip after four years. Typical prices: zip replacement 25 to 45 EUR, buckle or strap replacement 10 to 20 EUR, tent pole section 15 EUR, patch on a shell fabric 20 EUR. We quote before any work and the quote is binding. Repairs take two to three weeks including shipping, and return shipping is included in the price.

## Footwear

Footwear is covered for two years against sole separation, seam failure and membrane leaks that are not caused by punctures. Resoling is offered for selected full-grain leather boots at 89 EUR; ask support whether your model qualifies.
