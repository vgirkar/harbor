# Payments and security

## Accepted payment methods

We accept iDEAL, Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Klarna (pay in 30 days or in three instalments). Bank transfer is available for orders above 500 EUR; the order ships once the transfer arrives, usually within two working days. All prices are shown in euros and include VAT. Customers in Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom are charged in euros and their bank applies the conversion.

## When you are charged

Card, iDEAL, Bancontact and wallet payments are captured when you place the order. PayPal is captured at order time as well. Klarna charges nothing at checkout; the invoice is due 30 days after dispatch, or in three monthly instalments for the instalment option. If an item in your order becomes unavailable we refund that line immediately and ship the rest.

## Pending or duplicate charges

If your bank shows two charges for one order, one of them is almost always a pre-authorisation that the bank releases automatically within three to five working days. If both charges are still visible after five working days, send us the order number and the last four digits of the card and we ask our payment provider to trace it; a genuine duplicate is refunded within two working days of the trace.

## Discount codes and gift cards

One discount code can be used per order and codes cannot be combined with each other, but a code can be combined with a gift card. Codes cannot be applied after an order is placed. Gift cards are valid for three years from the purchase date, can be used across several orders, and are not refundable for cash. If you forgot to apply a code, contact support within 24 hours of ordering and we apply it manually as a partial refund.

## Security and fraud checks

Payments are processed by a PCI DSS level 1 certified provider; we never see or store full card numbers. A small number of orders are selected for a manual fraud check, which can delay dispatch by one working day. If we need to verify your identity we email you from our official support address and never ask for your full card number or password.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is attached to the dispatch email and is available in your account under the order. Business customers can enter a VAT number at checkout; intra-EU deliveries to a valid VAT number are invoiced without Dutch VAT.
