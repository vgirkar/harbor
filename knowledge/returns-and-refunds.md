# Returns and refunds

## Return window and conditions

You can return any item within 30 days of the delivery date, no reason needed. Items must be unused, in their original packaging with all tags attached. Footwear must be tried on indoors only; worn soles are not accepted. Underwear, base layers worn against the skin, opened food items, gas canisters and gift cards cannot be returned for hygiene or safety reasons unless they are faulty.

Items bought during a sale or with a discount code can be returned under the same 30 day rule. Personalised items (embroidered names, custom lengths) cannot be returned unless faulty.

## How to start a return

Log in to your account, open the order and click "Return items", or use the return form linked in your delivery email. You receive a prepaid return label by email within a few minutes. Pack the items, attach the label, and hand the parcel to any PostNL or DHL point within 14 days of creating the return. Returns from the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France, Austria, Denmark and Luxembourg are free. Returns from the rest of the EU cost 6.95 EUR, deducted from the refund. Returns from outside the EU are at your own cost and you must mark the parcel as "returned goods" on the customs form.

## Refund timing and method

Once the return reaches our warehouse we inspect it within three working days and then issue the refund. Your bank or card provider needs a further two to five working days to show the money. Refunds always go to the original payment method; we cannot refund to a different card or bank account. If you paid with Klarna, the Klarna invoice is adjusted and any amount already paid is refunded by Klarna. Gift card payments are refunded to a new gift card.

If an item is returned outside the 30 day window, or arrives used, we contact you and either send it back to you at our cost or offer a partial refund of up to 50 percent.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want. If you need the new item urgently, place the new order right away; the refund for the return follows separately. If the item is faulty, see the warranty policy instead: faulty items are replaced or repaired without using the 30 day return window.

## Returning a gift

Gifts can be returned by the recipient with the gift receipt. The refund goes to the original buyer's payment method or, if the recipient prefers, to a gift card in the recipient's name.
