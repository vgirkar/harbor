# Store, support hours and contact

## How to reach us

Support is available by chat on the website, by email, and by phone. Chat and phone are staffed Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 CET and Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00 CET; the AI assistant answers chat at all other times and hands over to a person when needed. Emails are answered within one working day, usually faster. Phone support is in Dutch and English; email support is also offered in German and French.

## Escalating to a human

Ask the assistant for a person at any time and it creates a support ticket with the conversation summary attached. Tickets about payments taken twice, suspected account compromise, safety issues or legal matters are marked high priority and answered within four working hours during support hours. Other tickets are answered within one working day.

## Store in Rotterdam

Our flagship store and repair workshop is in Rotterdam. Opening hours are Monday to Saturday 10:00 to 18:00 and Sunday 12:00 to 17:00. You can collect online orders in store for free: choose "collect in store" at checkout and we email you when the order is ready, usually the next working day. Collection orders are held for 14 days. Returns can also be handed in at the store for an immediate refund to the original payment method.

## Business and bulk orders

Clubs, schools and companies can request a quote for orders of 20 items or more; bulk orders get between 10 and 20 percent discount depending on volume and are invoiced with a 30 day payment term.
