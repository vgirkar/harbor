# Account and privacy

## Creating and managing an account

An account is optional; you can order as a guest. With an account you can track orders, start returns, save addresses and keep your warranty records. You can change your email address, password and addresses from the account settings page. Password resets are sent by email and the reset link is valid for one hour.

## Deleting your account and your data

You can request deletion of your account from the privacy section of the settings page or by emailing support from the account email address. We delete the account within 30 days. Order and invoice records are kept for seven years because Dutch tax law requires it, but they are detached from your account and no longer used for anything else.

## What data we store and why

We store the data needed to deliver orders and handle returns and warranty claims: name, addresses, email, phone number for the carrier, order history and support conversations. Payment details are held by our payment provider, not by us. Support chats with the assistant are stored for 12 months to improve answers and are never shared with third parties.

## Data access and export

Under the GDPR you can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you. Email support from your account address and we send a machine readable export within 30 days.

## Newsletter and marketing

The newsletter is opt-in. Every email has an unsubscribe link and unsubscribing takes effect immediately. We do not sell or rent customer data. Product review invitations are sent 14 days after delivery and can be switched off in your account.

## Security

Accounts are protected with a password of at least ten characters and optional two factor authentication through an authenticator app. If you suspect your account has been accessed by someone else, change your password immediately and contact support so we can check recent orders and addresses.
