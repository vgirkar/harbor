# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery options and costs

Harbor Outfitters ships from our warehouse in Rotterdam, Netherlands. Standard delivery inside the Netherlands and Belgium costs 4.95 EUR and is free on orders above 75 EUR. Standard delivery to Germany, France, Austria, Denmark and Luxembourg costs 7.95 EUR and is free above 120 EUR. Delivery to the rest of the European Union costs 12.95 EUR. We also ship to the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway for 19.95 EUR; customs duties and import VAT for those destinations are collected by the carrier on delivery and are not included in our prices.

Express delivery (next working day inside the Netherlands and Belgium, two working days for Germany and France) costs 9.95 EUR and must be ordered before 15:00 CET.

## Delivery times

Standard orders placed before 15:00 CET on a working day leave the warehouse the same day. Typical transit times after dispatch: Netherlands and Belgium one to two working days, Germany, France, Austria, Denmark and Luxembourg two to three working days, the rest of the EU three to five working days, United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway four to seven working days. During sale periods and the weeks before Christmas, dispatch can take up to two extra working days.

## Carriers and tracking

We ship with PostNL inside the Netherlands and Belgium and with DHL for every other destination. Every parcel is tracked. You receive a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel is scanned by the carrier, usually the evening of the dispatch day. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. If the tracking has not updated for five working days, contact support and we open an investigation with the carrier; investigations take up to ten working days, after which we send a replacement or refund.

## Missed deliveries and pickup points

If you are not home, PostNL and DHL attempt delivery once more on the next working day and then hold the parcel at a nearby pickup point for seven days. After seven days the parcel is returned to us and we refund the order minus the original shipping fee. You can choose a pickup point at checkout for the same price as home delivery.

## Damaged or lost parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the outer box and the contents before unpacking further and send the photos to support within 48 hours of delivery. We send a replacement or a full refund; you do not have to wait for a carrier investigation. A parcel counts as lost when tracking shows no movement for ten working days or the carrier confirms the loss. Lost parcels are replaced or refunded in full, including shipping.
